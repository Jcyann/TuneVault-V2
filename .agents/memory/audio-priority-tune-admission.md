---
name: Audio-priority tune admission
description: Product rule for deciding when a tune may be added before a verified recording is available.
---

Audio-backed tunes should always be sourced and processed before no-audio additions, but verified audio is not an absolute admission requirement. A useful, high-demand tune may be added without a recording when it is clearly marked as awaiting audio; never invent, guess, or attach an unverified recording to make the page look complete.

**Why:** The library should stay audio-centered without withholding otherwise valuable tunes and their notation or historical context.

**How to apply:** Keep strict title/source verification for every recording. Use popularity or demand as the gate for no-audio additions, surface the audio gap on the tune page, and prioritize those gaps in subsequent sourcing passes.
