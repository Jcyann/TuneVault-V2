name: Teacher credibility language
description: How TuneVault should establish Jason Chinchen as a legitimate teacher without generic trust language.
---

Establish Jason’s legitimacy through specific, supportable evidence: teaching experience, decades in traditional music, instrument and level focus, practical method, authentic media, and TuneVault itself as evidence of depth. Do not describe the desired visitor reaction as “trusting Jason” or use vague trust-building language in plans or site copy.

Keep Jason’s name and personal teacher profile on the Lessons page only. Earlier funnel pages may promote private lessons and explain their tune-based value, but should not name him.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected framing the funnel around whether a visitor “trusts Jason.” The intended effect is for legitimacy to be self-evident from concrete facts.

**How to apply:** On the homepage and tune pages, describe the lesson offer without Jason’s name. On the Lessons page, lead with verified specifics and transparent process. Never fabricate testimonials, credentials, student counts, or outcomes.
